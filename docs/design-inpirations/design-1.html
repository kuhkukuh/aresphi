The work — in passing 01 / 04
The Inner Room A chamber that holds its breath — concrete, shadow, and a single seam of daylight.
Poured Light Light, poured and left to set into the grain of the wall.
The Long Corridor A passage that forgets its own length between two distant apertures.
Quiet Mass Concrete taught to carry its weight without ever announcing it.

Principles / Read slowly

Restraint is not absence. It is the discipline of removing everything that does not carry weight, until what is left can stand for a century without apology — concrete that remembers the hand that poured it, light that arrives once a day and is enough.
